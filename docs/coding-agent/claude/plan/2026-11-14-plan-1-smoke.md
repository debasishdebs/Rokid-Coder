#### Finding: a permission answered at the laptop does not close the hook
- Prompt `echo hi2 > y.txt`; the alert arrived; I answered "1. Yes" in the pty. The command ran (`y.txt` = `hi2`).
- Claude did not close the hook connection. The `hook-forward.js` processes for the PermissionRequest stayed alive (`ps` showed it 31 s later). agentd logged no `approval_local`. The approval alert kept being re-sent, and stayed until the wait limit: `approval_resolved outcome=timeout` and `alert_closed reason=expired` at +2:00 (Y=120 s here, 30 min by default).
- The `done` alert of the same turn did not reach the phone until the approval expired (one alert at a time), so the wearer saw a dead approval and then a late "done".
- The session also stayed `status=needs_you` after the Stop (the permission `Notification` sets a pending request that nothing clears; Stop keeps `needs_you` when one is pending). After a phone-approved permission the same happened: `needs_you` through the Stop until the next prompt.
- Root cause: `ApprovalManager.request` assumes the forwarder's connection drops when Claude is answered at the laptop ("it drops when Claude gives up on the hook"). Live, it does not. The plugin registers no `PostToolUse`, so agentd gets no signal that the tool ran. Not fixed here (design-level: add `PostToolUse` to `plugins/claude-code/hooks/hooks.json`, resolve the matching pending approval as `local` and clear the permission pending request). Reported as a concern.

### Bug found and fixed in this task
- The `done` alert body was the previous turn's text in 2 of 3 turns (`body=done` for the BANANA turn, `body=BANANA` for the Pear turn): it read `lastAssistantText` from the transcript tail, and Stop fires before the final message is written. The Stop hook payload carries `last_assistant_message` (per the compat notes). `SessionStore.handleHook` now takes it for the `Stop` event. Regression test added in `agentd/test/session-store.test.js`; commit `cbaa15f6`. Re-verified live after the restart: prompt `Reply with only the word: KIWI` produced `alert kind=done body='KIWI'`.

### Other observations (not fixed)

**Setup.**
- agentd ran from `agentd/dist/cli.js run`, with temp `NEXUS_AGENTD_STATE_DIR` and `NEXUS_AGENTD_CLAUDE_DIR`, `rokidCoder.stateDir` in a second temp dir, `waitLimitSec=120` and Codex disabled.

- Two inputs in total: `/rokid:on`, then one prompt: `Run exactly this bash command and nothing else: echo hi > x.txt . Then reply with only: done`.

**Hooks agentd received, in order:**
1. `SessionStart`
2. `UserPromptExpansion` (`/rokid:on`)
3. `UserPromptSubmit`
4. `PreToolUse` Bash
5. `PermissionRequest` Bash. The approval was held, and the phone got `approval_request` and an `alert kind=approval`.
6. `Notification` ("Claude needs your permission", about 6 s later)

I then answered **3. No** in the dialog. The TUI showed `Interrupted · What should Claude do instead?`. **No further hook fired:** no `PostToolUse`, `PostToolUseFailure`, `PermissionDenied` or `Stop`.

**Outcome.** Claude closed the PermissionRequest hook's connection when "No" was chosen. Unlike "Yes" in the earlier finding, the forwarder process exited.
- agentd logged `approval_local {"reason":"hook_closed"}` 0.7 s after the keypress.
- The phone got `approval_resolved outcome=local` and `alert_closed reason=withdrawn`. The approval settled at once. `x.txt` was not created.

**Residue.**
- The session stayed `pendingRequest.kind=permission`, because the turn was interrupted, so no `Stop` fired.
- The interrupt also produced a transcript error: `status=error`, and an `alert kind=error body="user"` on the phone.
- The R68 `UserPromptSubmit` backstop clears the pending request on the next prompt. It was not exercised live, to stay within two prompts.

**Teardown.** Claude, the pty runner, the fake phone, the proxy and agentd were all killed. No `hook-forward.js` processes were left. `~/.rokid-coder` does not exist.
