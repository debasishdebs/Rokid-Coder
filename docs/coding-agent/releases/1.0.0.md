# Rokid Coder 1.0.0

Rokid Coder puts your Claude Code and Codex sessions on your Rokid glasses: permission prompts, questions and progress on the HUD, answered from the glasses by touch or voice.

Rokid Coder has no hosted backend or telemetry and collects nothing. Data leaves your devices only through services you already use: the Claude/Anthropic and Codex/OpenAI command-line tools, your Android speech engine, and Tailscale if you choose it. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/debasishdebs/Rokid-Coder/blob/release/1.0.0/PRIVACY.md).

## Install

Start on your phone. The app walks you through the rest.

1. **Phone.** Download `rokid-coder-1.0.0.apk` on the phone and open it. The phone app carries the glasses app and installs it on your glasses over the Rokid link, with no computer and no adb.
2. **Computer.** Download the installer for your computer and run it:
   - macOS 13 or later: `rokid-coder-1.0.0-macos-arm64.dmg` (Apple silicon) or `rokid-coder-1.0.0-macos-x64.dmg` (Intel).
   - Windows 11 22H2 or later with WSL 2: `rokid-coder-1.0.0-windows-x64.exe`.
3. **Pair.** The last step of the computer setup, and the phone's setup, show a code. Confirm on the phone that both show the same code.

### The installers are not signed yet

Your computer warns you the first time. This is expected for 1.0.0.

- **macOS:** right-click the `.pkg` inside the disk image and choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
- **Windows:** in the SmartScreen warning, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Each installer checks your system first and tells you how to fix anything missing. Windows needs WSL 2, and the installer checks it for you.

## What is new in 1.0.0

- One phone app, Rokid Coder, with a guided setup: permissions, the glasses app, the computer, pairing and a test alert.
- The glasses app installs from the phone, over the Rokid link.
- Every new pairing is confirmed by a code you compare on the phone, on every path, including the QR and pairing-line fallback. Links between your computer and phone are pinned, mutually authenticated TLS.
- Installers for macOS and for Windows (into WSL 2) that check first, set up the plugins for Claude Code and Codex, and roll back on a failed upgrade.
- A Tailscale connector for using Rokid Coder away from home.
- Settings, About shows the version and the privacy note.

## Upgrading

- **Computer, from 0.8.0:** run the installer. Setup keeps your computer's state, moves the plugins to the installed version and retires the old background job. `rokid-setup rollback` returns to the previous install.
- **Phone and glasses:** Rokid Coder 1.0.0 is signed with a new key and has its own application ids, so it installs as a new app next to any earlier build. Pair your computer with it again.
- **Rokid Nexus:** if the upstream Rokid Nexus app is installed, open it and tap Stop hub before Rokid Coder connects to your glasses. Only one app can hold that link.

## Known limits

- On Windows, `rk claude` and `rk codex` print a hint to use plugin mode (`/rokid:on`) until the launcher is verified there.
- Without WSL mirrored networking, automatic discovery does not work: pair with the QR code or Tailscale.
